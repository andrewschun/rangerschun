    Metric definitions & sources +

    Bat speed is recorded in mph; attack angle is the bat path angle at contact in degrees; time to contact is recorded in seconds. Rotational acceleration uses g, and on-plane efficiency uses percent. Additional fields retain the units shown in Blast.

    Record the same bat profile and drill context when assessing change. These sensor metrics alone do not measure bat-to-ball skill or swing decisions. Attack angle also depends on contact point (supplied Hitting manual, PDF p. 102).

        01 / START WITH THE PLAYER

        Assess, then choose.

        Use an individual baseline to identify a useful development priority. A roster spot or age group doesn’t select a program.

        02 / MAKE THE TASK CLEAR

        Give each rep a job.

        Choose the drill, implement, and feedback for a purpose. Focus on what the athlete is trying to accomplish.

        03 / SEE THE WHOLE WEEK

        Coordinate the work.

        Throwing, hitting, lifting, games, and recovery share the same training calendar. More work isn’t a plan by itself.

        Look for transfer.

        Check whether a change holds up under the demands of baseball. A drill record is one piece of the picture.

        KNOW THE SOURCE

        A reference, with the
        details kept intact.

        Observed · whole mphInputs from 70 through 86 mph show the exact displayed values transcribed from your 17 supplied calculator screenshots.

        Interpolated · decimal mphWithin that range, decimal inputs use a straight-line estimate between neighboring screenshots, rounded to one decimal. This is an estimate, not the original Driveline formula.

        Exact source coverage: 70–86 mphThe previous 80–86 mph projections have been replaced by screenshot observations. Inputs outside this range receive no targets. The original calculator formula has not been reconstructed.

        Colors and blanks matterEvery original ball color, drill label and blank is retained in Driveline view. The separate DUBS 150 g projections do not replace any screenshot observation.

        Equipment stays specificThese are Driveline reference observations. DUBS view copies Driveline references for 100, 225, 350, 450, and 1,000 g nominal matches. These are not validated DUBS output targets. Your supplied chart identifies orange as 350 g. The dark green DUBS 150 g row has four mass-interpolated projections: 60% of the gray 100 g reference plus 40% of the red 225 g reference. Roll-Ins are not projected. No yellow 150 g source readings or measured DUBS readings validate these projections.
